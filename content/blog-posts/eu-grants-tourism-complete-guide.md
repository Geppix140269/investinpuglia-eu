## Mini PIA: Your Gateway to Italian Tourism Investment

### Program Overview

The Mini PIA (Piccoli Investimenti per l'Accoglienza) represents Italy's most accessible EU-funded tourism grant program, specifically designed for investments between €100,000 and €500,000. Managed at the regional level, each program is tailored to local tourism development priorities.

**In Puglia specifically, Mini PIA 2024-2027 offers:**
- Grant amounts: €50,000 - €500,000
- Funding percentage: Up to 60% of eligible costs
- Application deadlines: Rolling basis until funds exhausted
- Processing time: 90-120 days from submission

### Eligible Project Types

## Beyond Mini PIA: Large-Scale Tourism Funding Opportunities

### FESR Tourism Development Program

For investments exceeding €1 million, the European Regional Development Fund offers the most substantial tourism financing in Italy:

**Program Highlights:**
- Grant amounts: €500,000 - €2,000,000
- Funding percentage: Up to 50% of eligible costs
- Focus areas: Integrated tourism development, sustainability, digitalization
- Application cycles: Bi-annual calls (typically March and September)

**Ideal Projects:**
- Resort developments (100+ rooms)
- Integrated tourism complexes
- Major historic property restorations
- Theme parks and entertainment venues
- Conference and exhibition centers

### National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR) Tourism Component

Italy's €191.5 billion PNRR includes €6.68 billion specifically allocated for tourism, focusing on digital transformation and sustainability:

**Key Funding Streams:**
- **Digital Tourism Hub Development:** €1.2 billion for technology integration

- **Accessibility Improvements:** €900 million for inclusive tourism facilities
