# Realistic Midjourney Video Prompts for Puglia Properties

## Understanding Midjourney Video Limitations
- Videos show **ONE SCENE** with camera movement (no transformations)
- Best for: Drone shots, dolly movements, orbital pans
- Duration: 4-5 seconds of smooth motion
- We'll create PAIRS: "Before" videos (ruins) + "After" videos (completed)

## TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS

### Camera Movements That Work:
- **Drone approach** - Flying towards property
- **Orbital pan** - Circling around subject
- **Dolly forward** - Moving straight ahead
- **Crane up/down** - Vertical reveal
- **Tracking sideways** - Moving parallel to subject

### What DOESN'T Work:
- ❌ Transformations (ruins becoming hotels)
- ❌ Time-lapse construction
- ❌ Before/after in same video
- ❌ Multiple locations in one video
- ❌ Complex narratives

### Lighting for Mood:
- **Golden hour** - Luxury, warmth, romance
- **Blue hour** - Elegance, exclusivity
- **Bright daylight** - Energy, activity, summer
- **Overcast** - Moody, abandoned (for "before" shots)

### Style Modifiers:
- `photorealistic` - More believable
- `cinematic` - Film-like quality
- `architectural photography` - Professional property shots
- `luxury travel photography` - Lifestyle focus
- `--style raw` - Less artistic interpretation
