# Mini PIA Turismo - Complete Process Guide for Foreign Investors

## Overview
The Mini PIA Turismo (Piccoli Investimenti Produttivi) is a grant program by Regione Puglia supporting tourism accommodation investments with non-refundable grants of 45-55% of eligible costs.

## Step-by-Step Process for Foreign Investors

### Phase 1: Pre-Application Preparation (3-6 months before)

#### 1. Company Setup Requirements
- **Establish Italian Entity**: Foreign investors MUST establish an Italian company (SRL, SPA, or SNC)
- **Register in Puglia**: Company must have operational headquarters in Puglia
- **Chamber of Commerce**: Register with local Camera di Commercio
- **Tax Registration**: Obtain Italian VAT number (Partita IVA) and fiscal code
- **ATECO Code**: Register with tourism-related ATECO codes (55.10, 55.20, 55.30)

#### 2. Property Acquisition
- **Property Purchase**: Secure preliminary purchase agreement (compromesso)
- **Due Diligence**: Complete technical and legal property surveys
- **Urban Planning**: Verify property zoning allows tourism use
- **Building Permits**: Obtain preliminary permits for renovations

#### 3. Project Development
- **Technical Project**: Develop detailed architectural and engineering plans
- **Business Plan**: Prepare 5-year business plan showing:
  - Investment breakdown
  - Revenue projections
  - Employment creation (minimum 3 ULA)
  - Market analysis
- **Environmental Assessment**: Conduct environmental impact studies if required

### Phase 2: Application Submission

#### 1. Required Documentation
**Company Documents:**
- Certificate of incorporation
- Chamber of Commerce registration (Visura Camerale)
- Financial statements (if existing company)
- Bank guarantee capability letter

**Project Documents:**
- Detailed technical project (signed by registered architect/engineer)
- Building permits or permit applications
- Property ownership/lease documents (minimum 10 years)
- Cost estimates from suppliers (minimum 3 quotes per item)
- Business plan with economic-financial projections

**Legal Compliance:**
- Anti-mafia certification
- Declaration of no pending bankruptcies
- Tax compliance certificates
- Social security compliance (DURC)

#### 2. Online Application Process
1. **Register on Sistema Puglia**: Create account on www.sistema.puglia.it
2. **Digital Signature**: Obtain Italian digital signature (firma digitale)
3. **Submit Application**: Complete online forms during application window
4. **Upload Documents**: Attach all required documentation
5. **Protocol Number**: Receive submission confirmation and protocol number

### Phase 3: Evaluation Period (60-90 days)

#### 1. Technical Evaluation
- Regional commission reviews technical feasibility
- Site inspections may be conducted
- Additional documentation may be requested

#### 2. Scoring Criteria
Projects are scored on:
- **Innovation**: Technology and digital systems (max 15 points)
- **Environmental**: Energy efficiency measures (max 15 points)
- **Employment**: Job creation above minimum (max 10 points)
- **Location**: Investments in smaller municipalities (max 10 points)
- **Accessibility**: Facilities for disabled guests (max 5 points)

**Minimum Score Required: 30/100 points**

### Phase 4: Approval and Contract

#### 1. Grant Approval
- Receive formal approval letter (Determina di Concessione)
- Grant amount confirmed (45-55% of eligible costs)
- Maximum grant: €2,000,000

#### 2. Contract Signing
- Sign disciplinary agreement within 30 days
- Provide bank guarantee if required
- Accept monitoring obligations

### Phase 5: Project Implementation

#### 1. Implementation Timeline
- **Start Works**: Within 6 months of approval
- **Complete Project**: Within 18-24 months
- **Extensions**: Maximum 6-month extension with justification

#### 2. Eligible Expenses
**Property and Construction (100% eligible):**
- Building purchase
- Renovation and restructuring
- Systems installation (electrical, plumbing, HVAC)
- External works and landscaping

**Equipment and Furnishings:**
- Furniture and fixtures
- Kitchen equipment
- IT systems
- Booking and management software

**Professional Services (max 10% of total):**
- Design and project management
- Technical studies
- Permits and authorizations

**Innovation/Environmental (minimum 5% required):**
- Renewable energy systems
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Digital check-in systems
- Smart room controls

#### 3. Payment Process
**Advance Payment (40%):**
- Request after signing contract
- Requires bank guarantee
- Paid within 30 days

**Progress Payments:**
- Submit expense reports quarterly
- Include paid invoices and bank transfers
- Technical progress reports required

**Final Payment (20%):**
- After project completion
- Final inspection by regional officials
- Submission of final report

### Phase 6: Post-Investment Obligations

#### 1. Operational Requirements
- **Start Operations**: Within 3 months of completion
- **Maintain Activity**: Minimum 5 years continuous operation
- **Employment**: Maintain promised employment levels
- **No Asset Sales**: Cannot sell grant-funded assets for 5 years

#### 2. Monitoring and Reporting
- **Annual Reports**: Submit operational and financial reports
- **Inspections**: Accept regional monitoring visits
- **Documentation**: Maintain all project records for 10 years
- **Publicity**: Display EU and regional funding acknowledgments

### Key Success Factors for Foreign Investors

#### 1. Local Partnerships
- Partner with Italian professionals (architects, engineers, accountants)
- Engage local construction companies familiar with regulations
- Use specialized grant consultants for application preparation

#### 2. Language Requirements
- All documentation must be in Italian
- Official translations required for foreign documents
- Certified translations with apostille for non-EU documents

#### 3. Financial Planning
- Budget 30-40% additional for non-eligible costs
- Account for VAT financing (22% on most expenses)
- Maintain liquidity for pre-financing expenses

#### 4. Timeline Considerations
- Total process: 18-36 months from application to operation
- Application windows: Usually 60-90 days (announced 30 days prior)
- Peak season alignment: Plan completion before summer season

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid

1. **Incomplete Documentation**: Missing documents = automatic rejection
2. **Underestimating Timelines**: Italian bureaucracy requires patience
3. **Inadequate Italian Entity**: Ensure proper company structure before applying
4. **Non-Compliant Quotes**: All estimates must be from registered Italian/EU suppliers
5. **Insufficient Innovation**: Projects need min. 5% innovation/environmental components
6. **Employment Miscalculation**: ULA calculation is complex - seek professional help
7. **Property Issues**: Ensure clear title and proper zoning before investing

### Professional Support Recommended

Given the complexity, foreign investors should engage:
- **Grant Consultant**: €15,000-25,000 (success fee often 5-8% of grant)
- **Technical Team**: Architect/Engineer familiar with tourism projects
- **Legal Advisor**: Italian lawyer for company setup and contracts
- **Accountant**: Italian commercialista for tax and financial compliance
